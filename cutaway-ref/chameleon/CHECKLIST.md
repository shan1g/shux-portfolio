# Chameleon checklist

Reference: `cutaway-ref/chameleon-reference.jpg` (704 x 435, a watermarked stock image, used only as a visual guide).
L = snout to tail base, about 320 px in the picture and 2.4 body units in the model. Every share below is of L,
measured off the picture.

1. **Silhouette and view:** a low, arch-backed robotic chameleon seen side-on from slightly in front and about 14 degrees above. Head on the left, four splayed legs on a flat surface, and a tail that droops behind into a spiral. Proportions: body depth 0.31, head (snout to the back of the head shell) 0.47, head height at the eye 0.32, tail uncurled about 1.0.
2. **Signature, the turret eye:** one per side. A conical graphite housing 0.27 across with a thick lip and 8 to 10 bolts round it, around a glossy blue-black lens 0.19 across that carries one sharp window highlight. Never a flat disc, never a white or glowing bezel.
3. **Head:** a tapered wedge with a blunt rounded snout tipped slightly down and a small bump on top. A smooth raised casque runs over the back of the skull, and a dark jaw seam runs from the snout back under the eye.
4. **Back:** 10 to 12 separate armour bands along an arched spine, with a dark gap of 0.008 to 0.01 between bands and a raised rib on every band forming the spine ridge. The spine's high point sits about 0.45 back from the neck. Never one smooth shell.
5. **Inlays:** 3 to 5 lighter grey square inlays, each about 0.055 across, on the rear flank bands only. None on the head, legs or tail.
6. **Legs:** 4 thin legs, 0.04 to 0.05 thick and 0.32 to 0.36 from hip to foot, splayed wide in a low crouch. The feet sit 0.1 or more out from the flank. Front elbows point back and out, hind knees forward and out.
7. **Joints:** a round bolted hub at every shoulder and hip (0.1 across, 6 bolts), plus smaller hubs (about 0.06) at the elbows, knees and wrists. Hub axes run across the leg's plane.
8. **Feet:** two opposed toe bundles of 2 curved toes each, gripping the surface. Never a single pad, a wheel or a blade.
9. **Tail:** 36 to 40 separate segments, tapering from 0.09 to 0.025 thick, that droop and then roll into a tight 1.5-turn spiral about 0.23 across, with the tip inside the spiral. The spiral's low point is near foot level. The segments never merge into a smooth tube.
10. **Materials:** dark satin graphite armour (value 0.12 to 0.2) with a clear coat that catches the stage's rim lights as thin edge lines. Black mechanism shows in the gaps, and only the bolt heads are steel. Never chrome, never white shells.
11. **Accent:** green only on the 3 small LEDs in a vertical column just behind each eye, each about 0.025 across. Nowhere else.
12. **Density:** measure the dark share inside the chameleon's silhouette (a hand mask on the reference). It must be at least 0.8 of the reference's; the reference robot is about 0.9 dark.
